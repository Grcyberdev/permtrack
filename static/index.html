Audit Rules & Reconciliation Protocol

1. Cross-Day Lookback: Pending indents from past 7 days are cross-referenced using canonical indent numbers. When a pass is later issued, it is categorized as a fulfilled carry-over with turnaround days tracked.

2. Bottle-to-Case Mathematics: Pack sizes $\ge 600\text{ml}\to 12\text{ b/cs}$, $\ge 350\text{ml}\to 24\text{ b/cs}$, $\ge 180\text{ml}\to 48\text{ b/cs}$, $<180\text{ml}\to 96\text{ b/cs}$. Total volume strictly equals $\text{Physical Cases} + \frac{\text{Loose Bottles}}{\text{Pack Bottles Per Case}}$.
